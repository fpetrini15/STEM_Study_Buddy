Send feedback

Report bugs, flag incorrect answers, or suggest improvements through GitHub Issues.

Before you open an issue

  1. Check whether a similar issue already exists.
  2. Note the subject, quiz or tool name, and which question or step had the problem.
  3. For bugs, include what you expected, what happened, and your browser if you can.

What to include

  • Bug report: steps to reproduce, expected result, and actual result.
  • Content error: the quiz name, question text (or a short quote), and why the answer or explanation looks wrong.
  • Feature idea: what you’d like to study or do, and why it would help.
